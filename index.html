BONUS SKILL POINTS: Pick any eight skills and add 20 points to each. This allows your Agent to specialize in certain professional skills or to learn a little about skills not included in his or her profession. You can even boost a single skill more than once, adding +20 each time. Unless the Handler says otherwise, you can't raise a skill higher than 80% during Agent creation.

Bonds

Bonds

Bonds indicate the strength of relationships with the most important people in your Agent's life, like a spouse, children, a partner, or a mentor. Bonds help Agents resist mental trauma—but as your Agent's life falls apart under the stress of Delta Green, Bonds deteriorate. Each Bond has a value that starts equal to your Agent's Charisma (CHA).

Live Play Sheet (Beta)

                                    

                                        Live Play is a purpose-built session theme designed for use at
                                        the
                                        table.
                                        It presents your agent's stats, skills, bonds, gear, and wounds on a single
                                        streamlined sheet
                                        with quick-roll buttons and a sticky tracker bar for HP, WP, and Sanity — no
                                        scrolling required mid-session.
                                    

                                    

                                        To use it, build your character on any theme as normal, then switch to Live
                                            Play
                                            (Field Notes)
                                        from the theme selector. Your stats, skills, and equipment will carry over
                                        automatically.
                                    

                                    

                                        ⚠ Beta feature: Because Live Play includes session-specific
                                        data
                                        (wounds, notes, tracker values, and free-form gear) that cannot be fully
                                        preserved
                                        by a share link alone, we've added JSON save files so you can keep a full
                                        offline
                                        backup
                                        of your character between sessions. Use Download Live Play after each
                                        session and
                                        Upload Live Play to restore it next time — nothing will be lost.
